Pricing & commitment

A fixed monthly management fee set by your total monthly ad spend: £349, £449, £599, £749, £899 or £1,199 a month for one platform, and £499, £649, £799, £999, £1,199 or £1,599 for Google + Meta, for ad spend up to £1,500, £2,500, £4,000, £7,500, £12,500 and £20,000. Above £20,000 a month, pricing is custom. Prices are set in £; € and $ are converted at the current rate. All prices are net.

Single-platform management covers one advertising platform: Google Ads or Meta Ads. Dual-platform management covers Google Ads and Meta Ads together, under the same total monthly ad-spend band — it does not need a bigger budget. Example: £5,000 a month on Google only is a single plan (£749); £3,000 on Google plus £2,000 on Meta is a dual plan (£999). Both are £5,000 of total ad spend. The dual fee is higher only because two platforms are actively managed.

Performance

Results depend on your market, product and budget — which is why we don't promise blanket timeframes. What we do promise: transparent data from day one, continuous optimisation, and honest communication about what's realistic in your industry. In the first call we set realistic expectations together.

It depends entirely on your industry and business model. In the free initial consultation we analyse your market and recommend a realistic minimum budget — based on competition, search volume and your goals. No flat figure, but an individual recommendation.

Month 1 builds the foundation (tracking, structure, learning phase), month 2 optimises on real data and month 3 scales what works. If something isn't performing, you see it plainly in the reporting and we discuss openly what to change — or whether stopping is the more honest recommendation.

After the consultation, audit and your approval, technical implementation begins: tracking setup, pixel and tag integration, and campaign build. This usually takes 7–14 business days. We only start once the technical foundation is solid.

Data & access

All campaign data stays in your own Google Ads and Meta Ads accounts — you keep full ownership. We work with access rights only, and you can remove that access at any time. You receive reporting as a monthly PDF with all KPIs.
