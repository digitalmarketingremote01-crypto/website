Setting up Google Ads — in the order that saves money

By Mohammad Danyal Shahzad · Updated on 15 August 2026

The right order is: create the account → set up billing → Google tag and conversion tracking → then the first campaign. Most guides start at the campaign, which produces the most expensive beginner mistake there is: buying clicks before you can measure them. Conversion status can take up to 48 hours to update after you install the tags.

Setting up Google Ads is not technically hard. What makes it expensive is the order. Start the campaign first and bolt tracking on afterwards, and you pay for the first week of traffic without knowing what it bought you. That week never comes back.

Step 1: Create the account — without a campaign

The signup flow pushes you into a first campaign, but Google explicitly allows skipping it: "At this stage, you also have the option to create an account without a campaign." (Google Ads Help)

The full route, including the manager-account option, is in the account without a campaign guide.

Step 2: Set up billing

On automatic payments nothing is charged in advance: "You're charged only after you've accrued advertising costs." (Google Ads Help)

When the charge actually lands depends on two triggers: "You'll be billed either 30 days after your last payment, or whenever your costs reach your billing threshold (the set amount that triggers a bill)." (Google Ads Help)

For bookkeeping, invoices and VAT work differently from a domestic supplier — see invoices and VAT. Paying by invoice is conditional, see monthly invoicing.

Step 3: Google tag and conversion tracking — before the first click

This is the step that gets skipped. The tag belongs on every page: "The Google tag snippet should be placed on all pages of your website" — typically, Google says, within the <head> (Google Ads Help).

The actual measurement depends on a second snippet: "For website conversion tracking to work, the event snippet should be installed on the conversion page itself." (Google Ads Help)

The 48-hour trap: "It may take up to 48 hours for the conversion status to update in Google Ads after implementing or correcting your tags." (Google Ads Help)

Install on launch day, check on launch day, and you may see nothing — then rip it out in a panic. Wait the 48 hours before judging. Details in the conversion tracking guide.

Step 4: Only now, the campaign

The campaign step itself is in the create a campaign guide.

What happens next — and how long it takes

Two waiting periods nobody can shorten:

  • Ad review, usually 24–48 hours, longer on new accounts — what to check if nothing runs.
  • Learning period, up to 3 weeks or 1–2 conversion cycles — learning period details.

In short: do not expect to judge anything in week one. Constant adjustments during that window keep restarting the learning period.
